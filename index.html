Expert guidance

Expert demonstrations are distilled into task guidance and tactile references. This context supports online decisions as the interaction unfolds.

The approach

We study how a general-purpose multimodal model interprets physical interactions and selects subsequent actions in contact-rich manipulation. We provide task guidance and tactile references distilled from expert demonstrations, together with visual–tactile observations and execution history. The model uses this context to assess the effects of previous actions through contact feedback and select subsequent advancing, probing, or corrective actions.
