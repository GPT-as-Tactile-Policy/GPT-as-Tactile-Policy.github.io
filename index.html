01 / Research overview

Understanding contact.
Choosing what comes next.

Contact-rich manipulation requires decisions about alignment, deformation, and changing contact conditions. Scene images offer global context; tactile observations reveal what happens at the gripper.

We study a direct-policy approach in which a general-purpose multimodal model combines expert task knowledge, visual–tactile observations, and execution history to select subsequent robot actions.

The central question is how task knowledge and contact feedback inform action decisions during interaction.

01

Expert guidance

Task principles and tactile references distilled from expert demonstrations.

02

Contact feedback

Visual and tactile observations that reveal the effects of executed actions.

03

Action decisions

Subsequent advances, probes, and corrections selected in the interaction loop.
